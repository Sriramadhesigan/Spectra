Why Spectra

Made for the moment between idea and pixel.

Every designer knows the feeling. You have the shape, the layout, the type. What you don’t have is the exact color — or the confidence it works. Spectra exists for that moment. No sign-up, no ads, no tabs fighting for your attention. Just the tools, exactly when you need them.

Zero friction

Everything runs instantly in your browser. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for, nothing you have to remember.
